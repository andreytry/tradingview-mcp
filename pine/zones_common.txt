ZONE CONSTRUCTION (identical in both strategies)
  base        up to 3 candles, each body <= 50% of the leg-out candle's body
  leg-out     body >= 1 x ATR(14) of its own timeframe
  BOS         leg-out closes beyond the prior swing high (demand) / swing low (supply)
  FVG         3-bar imbalance: high[2] < low (demand) / low[2] > high (supply)
  boundaries  zone top/bottom = high/low of the BASE candles
  proximal    the edge price returns from; distal = the far side
  built on    5m, 15m, 1h, 4h independently

CONFLUENCE
  >= 3 of the 4 timeframes hold an OVERLAPPING same-direction zone at the same price
  overlap, not containment
  the higher-timeframe zone must be confirmed BEFORE the entry-timeframe zone
